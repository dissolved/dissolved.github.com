description: Guidelines for constructive discussion on Code Dissolved.
---

Questions, corrections, and different approaches are welcome. Comments use [giscus](https://giscus.app/) and are stored publicly in this site's [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dissolved/dissolved.github.com/discussions). A GitHub account is required to participate.

- Be respectful. Critique an idea or implementation without attacking the person.
- Stay relevant to the post. Explain your reasoning and include software versions when reporting a technical issue.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not post credentials, private code, or personal information without permission.
- Do not post harassment, discrimination, spam, or malicious links.

I may edit or remove comments and restrict participation when needed to keep discussion useful and welcoming.
